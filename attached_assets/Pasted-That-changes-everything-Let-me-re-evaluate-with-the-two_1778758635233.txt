3. Revenue Attribution is the Only Moat
Without closed loop, bot is commodity. With it, you own the proof that AEO works.
What the bot must capture:
Source query: What did they search? (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google SGE, direct?)
Citation path: Which answer mentioned the client? (requires search app data)
Conversion event: Booked? Called? Dormant?
Revenue outcome: What did they pay? (monthly care fee, one-time service)
What the search app must report:
"You were cited 23 times this month for 8 queries"
"3 of those citations converted to €X revenue"
"Your competitor CarePlus is cited for '24h Pflege' but you're not — adding this content would unlock €Y"
