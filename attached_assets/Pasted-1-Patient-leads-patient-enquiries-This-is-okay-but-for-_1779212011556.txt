1. “Patient leads” / “patient enquiries”

This is okay, but for Meta/App Review I’d soften to customer enquiries or prospective patient enquiries where possible.

Better:

Capture and qualify prospective patient enquiries from WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook and your website.

2. “Books consultations directly into your calendar”

Fine, but make sure it is clearly booking/admin, not medical triage.

Better:

Helps eligible enquiries book consultations or request callbacks.

3. “Trained on healthcare and wellness protocols”

This is the riskiest line. It can sound like clinical protocol automation.

Better:

Trained on your services, FAQs, intake flows, pricing, availability, tone of voice, and escalation rules.

4. “Handles sensitive patient enquiries”

Also risky. It suggests the bot processes sensitive health information independently.

Better:

Handles enquiries with a careful, professional tone and escalates sensitive or clinical questions to your team.

5. “AI asks the right questions to understand each patient’s need”

Could be interpreted as medical intake/triage.

Better:

AI asks approved intake and qualification questions based on your business rules.

6. “Scores and qualifies every lead automatically”

Acceptable for sales, but in healthcare it should avoid sounding like care eligibility.

Better:

Scores commercial readiness and routes enquiries based on your rules.

7. “2-4× / 3× / 80% / 15-20%”

These are risky unless you can substantiate them. Meta may not care as much as ad platforms, but they look like hard performance claims.

Better:

Up to 2-4× more AI search appearances
Up to 80% of routine enquiries qualified automatically
Potential 15-20% increase in booked consultations

Or safer:

More AI search visibility
Faster lead response
Fewer missed enquiries
More qualified consultations

Safer app-review positioning

Use this language in Meta review and website legal/help pages:

GrowthMonk is a business communication and lead management platform for healthcare, wellness, medspa and service businesses. It helps businesses capture inbound enquiries from WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook and websites, qualify them using business-approved questions, route them to staff, and support appointment booking. GrowthMonk does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, treatment recommendations, prescriptions, emergency support, or clinical decision-making.
