Treatment procedure - Laser hair removal
Skin analysis & consultation: First, a personal consultation takes place, including a professional assessment of your skin type, hair density, and hair structure.
Cooling gel: Before starting, a pleasant, cooling gel is applied to protect the skin and make the treatment more comfortable, especially when used in the bikini area.
Individual laser adjustment: The laser is adjusted precisely to your skin and hair type.
Treatment: The laser light targets the hair roots precisely and destroys them without damaging the surrounding skin.
Duration: The sessions are usually short and can be planned around your daily routine. Smaller areas take about 10-15 minutes and larger areas 30-40 minutes.
Initial results: The treated hair falls out after just a few weeks. Hair growth is noticeably reduced with each session.
No downtime: You can resume your normal activities immediately after treatment and enjoy the benefits of effective hair removal.

How does laser hair removal work?
The laser emits controlled pulses of light that selectively absorb the melanin contained in the hair follicle, increasing the effectiveness of laser hair removal in Berlin.

Since the laser light is set to predominantly target the pigmented area of the hair, the surrounding skin usually remains unaffected. The treatment is particularly effective during the active growth phase of the hair (anagen phase), which is why several sessions are necessary to reach as many hair follicles as possible in this phase.

Since not all hairs are in the growth phase at the same time, hair regrowth may occur between treatments. This regrowth is normal and explains why several sessions are necessary to significantly reduce hair growth in the long term. The number of sessions required varies depending on the area of the body and hair type. The treatment is usually well tolerated; slight redness usually subsides within a few hours. For optimal and long-lasting results, it is important to consistently adhere to the recommended treatment intervals.
