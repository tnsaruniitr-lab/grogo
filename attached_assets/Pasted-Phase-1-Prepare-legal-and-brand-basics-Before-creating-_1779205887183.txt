Phase 1 — Prepare legal and brand basics

Before creating or verifying anything on Meta, prepare these.

Legal/business documents

Collect:

Dreamport Pvt Ltd registration certificate
PAN
GST details, if applicable
Business address proof
Business phone number
Business email, ideally: hello@growthmonk.ai
Website: https://growthmonk.ai
Website pages required

Create these pages on GrowthMonk:

1. Privacy Policy page

URL suggestion:

https://growthmonk.ai/privacy-policy

Must mention:

What data GrowthMonk collects
WhatsApp / Instagram / Facebook message data handling
Lead data handling
How users can request deletion
Contact email
Dreamport Pvt Ltd as operator

Suggested footer/entity line:

GrowthMonk is operated by Dreamport Pvt Ltd.

2. Terms of Service page

URL suggestion:

https://growthmonk.ai/terms

Must mention:

GrowthMonk provides AI search visibility, lead capture, CRM, WhatsApp/Instagram automation, and support automation services
Clients are responsible for having permission to message their users
GrowthMonk does not own client customer data
Usage must comply with Meta, WhatsApp, Instagram, and applicable data/privacy rules
Dreamport Pvt Ltd as legal operator
3. Data Deletion Instructions page

URL suggestion:

https://growthmonk.ai/data-deletion

Must mention:

Users/clients can request deletion by emailing hello@growthmonk.ai
Data deletion request should include business name, connected account, and email/phone
GrowthMonk will delete stored account tokens, lead records, message logs, and related connected account data where applicable
Give a reasonable deletion timeline, for example 7–30 days
4. Contact page

URL suggestion:

https://growthmonk.ai/contact

Must include:

Business name: Dreamport Pvt Ltd
Brand: GrowthMonk
Email: hello@growthmonk.ai
Business address, if comfortable/public
Support contact
Footer text to add across website

Use this:

GrowthMonk is operated by Dreamport Pvt Ltd.

This reduces mismatch risk because Meta will see Dreamport as the business owner and GrowthMonk as the brand/app.
