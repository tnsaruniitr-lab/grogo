Restore video hero playback on public demo websites.

Currently V3Hero switches from video to static image whenever ?preview=1 is present. That makes test/demo websites look worse because the video hero disappears.

Change behavior:
- Public demo pages should use the video hero by default.
- Do not disable video just because ?preview=1 is present.

- SSR HTML can keep a static semantic fallback for crawlers, but the hydrated user-facing page should restore the video.

Acceptance:
